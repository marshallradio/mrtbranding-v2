Recommended weights

Regular 400 — Body

Clear, neutral, and highly readable. The professional workhorse for body copy and long text.

Medium 500 — UI / Subheads

Adds presence without heaviness. Excellent for labels, navigation, and secondary headings.

SemiBold 600 — Strong headings

Crisp and authoritative. Often the sweet spot for headings that need weight but stay refined.

Bold 700 — Primary display

Maximum impact for hero headlines and key statements. Use sparingly so it stays powerful.

Color & contrast

M Blue headings on white.

Near-black body. This high-contrast pairing makes the typography stand out clearly. The existing color scheme is helping, not hurting.

M Red for accents only

Use red sparingly (eyebrows, CTAs, highlights). Overusing it reduces the crispness of the type.

Verdict

  • Crispest professional faces: MatterXH Regular + Medium + SemiBold + Bold.
  • Color scheme: It is helping. M Blue + near-black on white gives excellent contrast and lets the type feel vivid and premium. Keep red as a sparse accent.
  • Tracking: Negative letter-spacing on large sizes (-0.03 to -0.04em) is the biggest visual upgrade.
  • Avoid for body: Thin, Light, Heavy, and Black — they either look too delicate or too heavy for everyday professional use.
  • Fonts are currently in the repository root (paths updated). Moving them into a fonts/ folder is cleaner if you prefer.
