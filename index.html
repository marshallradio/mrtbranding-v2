The four faces

Light 300

Light is delicate and elegant. Best for large display or secondary text where a softer presence is wanted.

Regular 400

Regular is the workhorse for body copy. Clear, neutral, and highly readable at small sizes.

Medium 500

Medium adds presence for UI labels, subheads, and body text that needs a bit more weight without going full Bold.

Bold 700

Bold carries the brand voice for headings, buttons, and key statements. Use it sparingly so it stays powerful.

Suggestions

  • Biggest improvement: Hosting the real Matter faces instead of falling back to Helvetica/Arial.
  • Tracking: Negative letter-spacing (-0.03 to -0.04em) on display sizes makes it feel tighter and more premium.
  • Line-height: 0.9–1.05 for large display; 1.5–1.65 for body.
  • Color: M Blue (#1B3A6A) headings + near-black body gives strong, vivid contrast.
  • SemiMono note: This XH SemiMono variant has a slightly technical character. Test it against the regular Matter faces if you have both.
  • Original guidelines remain at marshallradio/mrtbranding.
